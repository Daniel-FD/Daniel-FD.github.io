On scoring things

A compliance risk score and an LLM classifier have something in common: both are judged on the cases they get wrong, and the two kinds of wrong rarely cost the same. Missing a risky flow is usually worse than sending an analyst one extra alert. So accuracy alone is the wrong headline: precision P (how many flags were right) and recall R (how many real cases were caught) have to be read together, and

Fβ = (1 + β²)·P·R / (β²·P + R) (1)

with β > 1 says out loud that recall matters more. Choosing β is a business decision, not a modelling one, and it is worth making before the model is built, on a labelled evaluation set that everyone agrees on.
