Abstract

I am a data scientist and AI engineer in London. As Lead Data Scientist at Range, I build data pipelines, machine-learning models and AI features for blockchain security and stablecoin compliance; Range monitors more than $30B in assets across 21 integrated chains. Before that I was Lead AI Engineer at HelpFirst AI, building award-winning LLM applications, including one that saved Citizens Advice Scotland £400k a year. I have also shipped data science in healthcare (patented computer-vision diagnostics) and travel (a recommender system), and I work part-time as a research associate at Imperial College London, using machine learning for biomarker discovery. My training is in engineering, up to a PhD at Imperial. This document covers current work (§1), selected projects (§2), positions (§3), education (§4) and publications (§5).

1Current work

1.1Blockchain analytics at Range

At Range [4] I lead machine-learning initiatives for blockchain security and stablecoin1A token designed to hold a steady value, usually one US dollar. compliance. Range monitors more than $30B in assets across 21 integrated chains. The work, at the level of skills:

  1. Multi-chain data pipelines on ClickHouse: real-time blockchain data pipelines feeding machine-learning models and APIs.
  2. ML risk scoring for addresses and transactions: real-time threat detection, anomaly detection and transaction risk scoring (KYT/AML) in DeFi.
  3. Sanctions and stablecoin compliance monitoring: risk analytics and cross-chain monitoring for stablecoin (USDC) compliance.
  4. LLM agents and AI features in production for compliance and security workflows.

Compliance, at its simplest, is bookkeeping on a transaction graph. Ignoring fees, mints and burns, the balance bi held at address i changes only by what flows in minus what flows out,

dbi/dt = Σj fj→i − Σj fi→j,(1)

where fj→i is the rate at which value moves from j to i. Much of the job is tracing the terms on the right-hand side of Eq. (1) back to where they came from.

1.2AI engineering

At Range I also build AI and LLM-powered features (§1.1). Before that, from 2022 to March 2025, I was Lead AI Engineer at HelpFirst AI:

  1. AI system for Citizens Advice Scotland. An award-winning solution: callers reach their local bureau in under a minute instead of waiting up to 24 hours for a callback. It saves £400k a year and won the 2023 ScotlandIS Tech for Good Award.
  2. Prompt engineering, systematic evaluation and fine-tuning to improve LLM performance, with frameworks for model assessment and for ethical, responsible AI in sensitive settings.
  3. Predicting customer vulnerability with NLP and large language models.
  4. AI-Prompt-Optimiser [5] (open source). A framework for optimising LLM prompts through automatic iterative refinement.

1.3Data science in industry and academia

In industry, I also led data science at Bryant Medical: patented AI for disease diagnosis and biometric measurement using computer vision, with diagnosis 60% faster. I led the ML, data engineering and cloud deployment, and handled hiring and training. At loveholidays I built a recommendation system with gradient-boosted trees and user clustering: an expected 2.8× in bookings, about £17M a year in revenue and over 10,000 new bookings. In academia, I am a part-time research associate at Imperial College London, applying machine learning to biomarker discovery in allergy and immunology trials.
